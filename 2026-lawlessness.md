# Lawlessness and a cast of obstreperous characters

What is a constitution? A defining article to help a populace organize themselves and govern their well-being as a collective. “Socialism!” I hear you cry but we are stronger as one. Where would the capital markets be without robust anti-worker regulation to ensure unconstrained profits for the masses of billionaires!

Society simply doesn’t function without this concept being respected and enforced, the stronger bully the weak which is something I personally can’t afford with my arm size. Lord of the flies, the 2012 NFL referee lockout, Liz Truss’s cabinet, the Reconstruction Era South, all are tragic cases of a rigorously defined constitution not being enforced and respected.

After head of league officiating, Clarissa Garber, received multiple concerns about the league constitution not being upheld, we have to be reminded of Abraham Lincoln’s finest hour, “A fantasy league divided against itself cannot stand”. Enduring advice that thankfully has outlasted his push to make top hats fashionable.

## A Constitutional Review

*Second Last*

*Weekly Forfeit*

The last place finisher each week during the regular season has to produce a piece of content for the league.
